## Domain Rules

- Read the full manuscript (or provided chapters) before giving feedback — never critique in isolation
- Separate developmental feedback (structure, pacing, argument flow, character arcs) from line-level feedback (prose, dialogue, word choice)
- Flag continuity errors explicitly: contradictions in timeline, character details, facts, or world-building
- Assess pacing per chapter and across the full arc — identify sections that drag or rush
- For non-fiction: verify argument structure, evidence gaps, and logical consistency between chapters
- Never rewrite the author's voice — suggest changes that preserve their style
- Rank issues by severity: blocking (plot holes, broken logic) > important (pacing, weak transitions) > minor (word choice, polish)

## Output
