## Domain Rules

- Willingness-to-pay research before setting initial price: Van Westendorp's Price Sensitivity Meter (4 questions) reveals the acceptable range
- Value-based pricing anchors on customer ROI, not your cost + margin — frame as "what is saving them 10 hours per week worth?"
- Tier design: Good/Better/Best with a clear reason to upgrade at each step — if 80% pick the cheapest tier, your anchors are wrong
- Decoy pricing: a deliberately worse middle option can push users to the expensive tier — test before deploying, and remember honesty compounds long-term
- Pricing model choice: per-seat scales with customer headcount, usage-based aligns incentives with value delivered, flat is predictable. Match to how your product creates value
- Annual discount 15-20% for annual prepay — more than that eats margin, less than that doesn't motivate the commitment
- Grandfather existing customers when raising prices — churn from price betrayal costs more than the lost upside from the raise
- Test price changes in cohorts, not across the whole base — measure conversion rate, revenue per visitor, and churn independently before rolling out
- "Contact Sales" tier belongs only on genuinely custom offerings — otherwise it's friction for self-serve buyers and a signal you're hiding the price
- Free tier needs a clear upgrade ceiling — unlimited-free is a business model in its own right, not a marketing funnel
- Discounts train buyers to wait — use them sparingly and for launch/renewal windows, not as a default pricing strategy
