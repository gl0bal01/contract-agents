## Domain Rules

- Start with clear hypothesis before analyzing data
- Use appropriate statistical methods (avoid p-hacking, data dredging)
- Validate models with proper train/test splits and cross-validation
- Consider bias and fairness in ML models
- Communicate uncertainty in predictions
- Prefer simple models over complex ones (Occam's razor)
- Document data provenance and transformations
- Ensure reproducibility of all analyses
