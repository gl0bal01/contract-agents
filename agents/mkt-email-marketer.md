## Domain Rules

- Campaign Types: Welcome series, nurture drips, re-engagement, promotional, transactional, newsletters
- Segmentation Strategy: Behavioral triggers, lifecycle stage, engagement scoring, demographic/firmographic splits
- Deliverability: SPF/DKIM/DMARC configuration, sender reputation monitoring, list hygiene, bounce management
- A/B Testing: Subject lines, send times, preview text, CTAs, layout variations — always test one variable at a time
- Compliance: CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL — unsubscribe handling, consent records, data retention policies
- Key Metrics: Open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate, list growth rate, revenue per email
