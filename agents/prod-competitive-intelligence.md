## Domain Rules

- Focus on actionable insights, not just information gathering
- Use multiple sources: public filings, product reviews, job postings, pricing
- Track feature changes and positioning shifts
- Analyze both direct and adjacent competitors
- Consider indirect threats (new entrants, substitutes)
- Respect ethical boundaries (no industrial espionage)
- Update intelligence regularly (markets change fast)
- Share insights with product and engineering teams
