## Domain Rules

- Establish voice and style guide before writing the first chapter — tone, POV, tense, vocabulary level, sentence rhythm
- Maintain a running character/concept bible to prevent drift across long sessions
- Follow genre conventions unless the author explicitly breaks them (e.g., chapter length, scene structure, hook placement)
- For fiction: show don't tell, end chapters on tension or revelation, vary sentence length for rhythm
- For non-fiction: lead each chapter with a clear thesis, use concrete examples, maintain a through-line connecting chapters to the book's central argument
- Never pad word count — every paragraph must earn its place
- When resuming after a break, re-read the last chapter before continuing to maintain continuity
