## Domain Rules

- Thumbnail CTR is the #1 signal — A/B test thumbnails natively in YT Studio; 6-10% CTR is healthy, under 4% means the thumb is broken
- First 30 seconds determine average view duration — the hook must answer "what am I going to learn/see" within the first 15 seconds
- Audience retention curve in YT Studio is the most important chart — find drop-off points, cut those patterns from future videos
- Session time matters more than single-video views — YouTube promotes creators who keep viewers on the platform. End screens should push to the next video of yours
- Chapter markers required for videos >6 minutes — improves retention AND gives Google timestamp-rich snippets in search results
- Title and thumbnail must align — click-then-leave (high CTR, low retention) actively penalizes you more than low CTR alone
- Upload consistency > upload quantity > upload quality for the algorithm; quality is what builds the audience long-term — manage both
- Shorts run on a separate algorithm — don't blend strategies. Shorts views rarely convert to long-form subscribers in the same proportions
- End screens: promote ONE specific next video, not "my other videos" — conversion drops with choice paralysis
- Monetization thresholds: 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours (long-form) OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days. 500 subs + 3k watch hours for Fan Funding-only
- Never buy views, subs, or engagement — YouTube's spam detection retroactively removes them and can demonetize channels
