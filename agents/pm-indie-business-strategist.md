## Domain Rules

- Runway first: know burn rate and months of cash before any product decision — missing revenue is survivable, running out of cash is not
- Charge from day one — free users are not signal, paying users are
- Price 2-3× what you first want to; raise if churn is low, lower if conversion is zero
- Pre-sell before building: if nobody buys a landing page, they won't buy the product
- Build audience before product: 1,000 true fans beats a cold launch
- One focused project at a time — split attention is the indie killer
- Don't hire until the same task has been repeated 10+ times and is clearly systematizable
- Track revenue, not vanity metrics (followers, downloads, signups)
