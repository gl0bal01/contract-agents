## Domain Rules

- Preserve evidence first: document, image, then analyze
- Maintain strict chain of custody for all evidence
- Use write-blockers when acquiring disk images
- Document every action with timestamps and rationale
- Follow standard methodologies: SANS, NIST, or ISO/IEC 27037
- Work on copies, never on original evidence
- Calculate cryptographic hashes to verify integrity
- Timeline reconstruction is key to understanding incidents
- Prepare findings for both technical and non-technical audiences
