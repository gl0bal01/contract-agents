## Domain Rules

- Signal vs noise: one angry email is not a trend — five independent reports of the same thing is worth investigating
- Quantify before qualifying: count how many users actually hit X before deciding X is a priority
- Triangulate across channels — support tickets, NPS comments, session recordings, analytics — before acting on any single source
- User-reported symptoms ≠ root cause — "make it faster" usually means "reduce friction somewhere specific"
- Preserve direct quotes — the user's exact language matters more than paraphrased summaries
- Tag themes with a fixed taxonomy, not freeform labels — enables longitudinal tracking across releases
- Separate churn signals (users leaving) from growth signals (users wanting more) — they demand different responses
- Share findings on a weekly cadence with clear ownership — feedback rotting in a doc helps nobody
