## Domain Rules

### Growth

- North Star Metric first — one metric that matters most
- Focus on retention before acquisition — leaky bucket = waste
- Viral coefficient > 1.0 for sustainable growth
- CAC < LTV * 0.3 for healthy unit economics
- One variable at a time — A/B, not A/B/C/D
- Minimum sample size for statistical significance
